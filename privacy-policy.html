Kontrst Lofi Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Kontrst Lofi Uploader is a small personal tool. It is used by one person, its owner, to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app can access

When the owner signs in with Google, the app asks for two permissions on the owner's own YouTube channel: to upload videos and thumbnails, and to view basic details of the channel (its name and ID) so the owner can check the right channel is connected. The app cannot delete videos, post comments, or manage the account.

What the app stores

The sign-in token that Google issues is stored in a private file on the owner's own computer. It is not sent anywhere except to Google, to upload videos and check the channel.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or share personal data about anyone other than its owner.
  • It does not sell data, show adverts, or use data for advertising or profiling.
  • It does not share Google user data with any third party.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the token file from their computer.
